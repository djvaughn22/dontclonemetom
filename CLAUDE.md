# DontCloneMeTom.com
Real rescue-dog campaign (Tom Brady cloned his dog → adopt originals). Secular. Accent: **#2DD4BF**.
- **Brand (owner, Sep 9 2026 — supersedes the Aug 9 lowercase lock):** every user-visible reference reads exactly **DontCloneMeTom.com** — CamelCase, one word, always with `.com`. Never lowercase `dontclonemetom.com`, never a spaced variant, never the bare name without `.com`. URLs stay lowercase (`https://dontclonemetom.com`) — this is branding and metadata, not URL casing. Machine keys are exempt and must not be touched: the npm package name, `DCMT_BRAND.brand` (the `utm_campaign` key), the `#dontclonemetom` hashtag, download-filename slugs, and the `dontclonemetom|rotation|<round>|<id>` shuffle key in `rotation.ts` (changing it reshuffles Dog of the Day). Guard: `app/lib/__tests__/brand.test.ts`.
- **Slogan (owner, Nov 7 2026):** **Don’t clone me. Rescue me.** — replaced “Don’t clone me. Adopt me.” everywhere it worked as the slogan (hero, share lines, site metadata). Hero subline: “Real dogs near you need homes today. Each one links straight to its rescue.” plus a “Find dogs near me” button to the search.
- **Homepage hero (Sep 9 2026):** the hero IS the Dog of the Day — heading, dynamic dog from `/api/dog-of-the-day`, photo, name, breed/age teaser, and a link straight to that dog's `/dogs/[id]`. It must NEVER `return null`: it renders a skeleton while loading and a `/today` fallback when the feed is unavailable, so the label never disappears. Isaiah/Batdog stays in the hero as a small mascot chip BELOW the featured dog and must never stand in for the Dog of the Day. Guard: `app/__tests__/dogOfTheDayHero.test.tsx`.
- The `pet-scroller` widget is keyless and scoped to St. Louis rescue org IDs (`LOCAL_RESCUE_ORGS`) — the keyless Petfinder graphql CANNOT filter by location any other way; do not retry.
- Personal-name exception (owner decision, Jul 2026): Isaiah — DJ's family dog — is public by name with his own profile at `/dogs/isaiah`. Featured card name (owner-set, Jul 30 2026): **Batdog**. "Bruzer Zayne" survives in the deck and is the ONLY correct spelling of that family name — "Bruce Zayne"/"Bruze Zayne"/"Bruiser" are test-banned (`BANNED_HERO_SPELLINGS`). No other personal names on the site.
- Fun Dog Trading Cards (Jul 30 2026, replaced the Dog Identity Engine + `/legend`; docs: `docs/DOG-TRADING-CARDS.md`, rollback tag `pre-trading-cards-2026-07-30`): one collectible card at a time — real photo, real name, ONE nickname, one short saying, the day, Share. Big button = **Spin a New Card**; the full nickname list never renders. All card data + pure logic in `app/lib/cards/tradingCards.ts` — small curated pools only, deterministic, no name mutations, no celebrity mashups. Kid maker at `/cards` (photo stays in the browser); `/legend` redirects there. Public language stays plain — "engine"/"generated"/"algorithm"/"intelligen" etc. are in `BANNED_PUBLIC_PHRASES`, test-enforced.
- Dog profiles are DATA: `app/lib/dogProfiles.ts` (DogProfileV1) renders through `app/components/profile/DogProfileView.tsx` on `/dogs/[slug]`; a profile's card deck is owner-curated in the record (`cards`, featured = `cards[0]`). New dog = one record + images. Slugs must never be all digits (numeric `/dogs/[id]` = live RescueGroups listings — those pages reuse the card with the universal deck + quiet rescue attribution). Cards may only riff on owner/rescue-supplied names/facts — `findUnverifiedClaims` + tests enforce it; never state temperament/medical/compatibility outside `verifiedFacts`.
- **Dog links (Aug 4 2026, docs: `docs/LINK-AUDIT-2026-08-04.md`):** every adoptable-dog link resolves through `app/lib/dogDestination.ts` (`resolveDogDestination` → `exact-dog` / `shelter-fallback` / `none` + honest label). "Meet [Name]" ONLY when the link opens that exact dog's page; fallbacks say "View shelter listings" / "Visit the rescue". Homepage tiles with a profileUrl are one-tap external links. Guard tests: `dogDestination.test.ts`, `dogLinkSurfaces.test.ts`. Never generate dog URLs from names.
- **Adoption integrity (Sep 6 2026, docs: `docs/ADOPTION-INTEGRITY-2026-09-06.md`):** "Last verified" used to be `new Date()` at render — MOO was Dog of the Day while her APA page said PET NOT FOUND. FRESHNESS IS TWO FIELDS AND MUST STAY TWO: `Dog.feedSeenAt` (upstream feed) vs `adoption.destinationVerifiedAt` + `destinationVerificationMethod` (real confirmation). A feed refresh may NEVER write the second, and no surface may print "Last verified" except through `freshnessLine()`. APA of Missouri is decided by their official feed (`app/lib/officialAvailability.ts`) — a petID's SHAPE is never proof; they migrated PetPoint→Shelterluv and retired all 29 `A#####` ids at once. HTTP 200 is not alive (`unavailableListing.ts` reads visible text, scripts stripped). Blocked/timeout = UNVERIFIED, never verified and never dead. Dog of the Day requires a confirmed destination. Identity conflicts (`identityIntegrity.ts`) are quarantined, never merged. All outbound checks go through `safeFetch.ts` (SSRF-screened, bounded); never impersonate a browser to beat bot protection. Daily audit: `/api/integrity-audit` + `npx tsx scripts/adoption-integrity-audit.ts`.
- Any ZIP works in-app (RescueGroups radius search), and so does **Use my location**: the browser's position is rounded to two decimals and POSTed to `/api/adoptable-pets` — never put in a URL, a shared link, or storage.
- **Dog of the Day rotation (Nov 7 2026, docs: `docs/DOG-OF-THE-DAY-ROTATION-2026-11-07.md`):** the old pick put audited dogs first and took the first after a date-seeded point — Karma won 18 of 60 days and 11 eligible dogs were never featured. `app/lib/rotation.ts` now runs ROUNDS: every eligible dog gets one turn before any dog repeats; inside a round a weighted shuffle gives longer listings a modest nudge (weight 1.0 → 1.5 at one year, never a queue); a dog featured in the last 7 days waits behind everyone; new listings join the round in progress. NEVER reintroduce “confirmed dogs first” — the live check decides only whether a candidate can take her turn. Memory: `.github/workflows/record-dog-of-the-day.yml` records the live pick daily to `dog-of-the-day.json` on the data-only `dog-of-the-day-history` branch (its own vercel.json disables deploys); `featuredHistory.ts` reads it (10-min cache, 2.5s timeout) and the rotation pins recorded days and fills gaps by running its rule forward from `ROTATION_EPOCH`. Guards: `rotation.test.ts`, `featuredHistory.test.ts`.
- **Not adoptable right now (Nov 7 2026):** `isPubliclyEligible` also drops a dog her rescue flags `isAdoptionPending`, a listing whose own name says adopted, and foster/volunteer sign-up records posing as dogs (“FOSTER”). The dog page still loads for anyone holding a link and says so plainly.
- **Listing dates:** `Dog.listedAt` is RescueGroups `createdDate` — a LISTING date, never intake or time in a shelter. It only ever renders as “Listed since …” / “New listing” (`app/lib/listingAge.ts`). Tiles tag only new (≤14 days) and year-plus listings.
- **Adoption path:** dog page = photo → facts → `AdoptionActions` (Meet [Name] ↗ + Email the rescue, one plain “what happens next” line) → story → share → spin → trading cards. Never invent fees, timing, availability or response times; the rescue’s own description may state theirs.
