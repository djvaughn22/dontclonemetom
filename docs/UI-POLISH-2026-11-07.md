# Homepage UI polish — Nov 7 2026

Owner brief: keep the rescue messaging and adoption path, make the page feel
designed — Isaiah as the personality, real dogs as the stars, navy and teal.

## What changed

- **Hero.** Isaiah's face and name open the page; "Don't clone me. Rescue me."
  is the H1 (the header already names the site, so the hero no longer repeats
  it). One line of copy, then "📍 Find dogs near me", which runs the location
  search below, with "or enter a ZIP" beside it. Desktop puts the intro and the
  Dog of the Day side by side; phones stack them with less space between.
- **Dog of the Day.** 4:3 photo across the card (object-cover, never
  stretched), then name, age · sex, breed, rescue · city, listed-since.
  "Meet [Name] ↗" is the one solid button and opens her exact rescue listing
  (`meet` in `/api/dog-of-the-day`, exact-dog only); photo and name open her
  page here. Share and Spin sit underneath. A spun dog is labeled "not today's
  pick" with "Back to [today's dog]"; today's pick is held separately and is
  never refetched, replaced or recorded by a spin.
- **Find a dog.** ZIP + distance + Search, then Use my location and a Filters
  fold. Sort and "Show 22 / 222 / All" moved next to the results. The count line
  says how many dogs are within the chosen distance and how many are farther.
  Loading shows tile skeletons; a "Show all N dogs" button ends a cut list.
- **Tiles.** Photo first, name + distance, age · sex, breed, rescue; "Meet ↗"
  and Details on one line.
- **Details sheet.** Bottom sheet on phones, solid photo controls, Escape
  closes it, and focus goes in and comes back.
- **Lower sections.** One card style; How adopting works + More ways to help
  side by side on desktop; "Pass it on" and "Share these lines" merged.
- **Dog page.** Photo beside the facts and "Meet [Name]" on desktop; on phones
  the photo stops at half the screen.
- **Global CSS.** The homepage 44px touch-target floor moved into
  `@layer base` (unlayered, it capped every taller button at 44px);
  `:focus-visible` no longer forces a 4px radius on pills; light theme darkens
  the focus ring. Every ↗ carries U+FE0E (text arrow, not an emoji box).

## Not changed

Rotation, history recorder, link integrity, the 222 baseline and widening,
URL params and saved settings, location privacy (rounded, POSTed, never in a
URL), shared nav/footer/theme (synced from the hub). The shared header was 680px
wide; since Nov 8 it lines up with the page (the hub's OpenMirrorNav reads
`--om-bar-max` / `--om-bar-pad-x`, which `app/globals.css` sets).
